# 🛡️ Auro Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 30, 2026

---

## 📋 1. What Data We Collect & Why

We keep data collection to an absolute minimum. We do not collect your real name, email, or any personal details. Here is exactly what we process to make the bot work:

* **Your Discord User ID:** Used to remember your earned profile badges and calculate your total listening hours for your right-click profile card.
* **Your Server's ID:** Used to save your server's custom prefix and remember which channels the bot is allowed to play music in.
* **Voice Timestamps:** Used to log the exact minute you join and leave a voice channel with Auro so the system can calculate your listening hours.

> 🛑 **Voice Privacy Guarantee:** Auro NEVER listens to, records, or stores your actual voice conversations. The bot only streams music—your private channel conversations remain 100% private.

---

## ⏳ 2. How Long We Keep Your Data

We follow strict data minimization rules so your information isn't kept longer than necessary:
* **Live Tracking Data:** Your temporary session clock is instantly deleted from our active cache files the exact second you leave the voice channel.
* **Lifetime Statistics:** Your total accumulated listening hours are stored locally on our host server solely to power your profile card lookup.

---

## ❌ 3. How to Delete Your Data

Your data belongs entirely to you, and you have full control over removing it:
* **Wiping Your Profile:** If you want your lifetime listening hours completely deleted from Auro's memory, join our Support Server and let our team know. We will flush your User ID from our database within 48 hours.
* **Wiping Your Server:** Server owners can instantly clear all server-specific settings (like custom prefixes) simply by kicking Auro from the server.

---

## 🔒 4. No Third-Party Sharing

We will **never** sell, rent, trade, or share your server stats, Discord IDs, or metadata with any advertising company, marketing agency, or outside network. Everything stays entirely within our secure, isolated host server.

---

## 💬 5. Support & Questions

If you want to request a copy of your saved stats, need a manual data wipe, or want to report a bug, we are here to help! 

Join our official **[Auro Support Server]** to chat directly with our team or open an infrastructure support ticket. Enjoy the music! 🎵
