## ⏱️ 3. Automatic Disconnection

Auro follows a standard idle rule to manage active connections:
* If a voice channel becomes empty and no human listeners remain, Auro will automatically pause playback.
* If the channel stays empty for **5 minutes**, the bot will automatically leave the channel. You can bring it back instantly at any time by running a new music command.
